About VeriStaff

We started VeriStaff because "outsourcing" had become a euphemism.

Too many providers hide where their staff sit and what they're paid. We built the opposite: an agency that puts that information in the contract, not behind a wall.

Our Story

Built by people who'd been on both sides of a bad outsourcing deal

VeriStaff's founders spent years watching two things happen at once: Australian small businesses got quoted "savings" that never materialised past hidden fees, and the offshore staff behind those contracts were paid a fraction of what clients believed.

So we rebuilt the model from the contract up — one placement fee, one fair wage, one named point of contact. No subcontracted layers in between.
